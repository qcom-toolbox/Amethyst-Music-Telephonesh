Privacy Policy

App: Amethyst Music  •  Last updated: August 15, 2026

Amethyst Music ("the app") is an open-source client application that connects to a self-hosted music server (compatible with Purple Music or Amethyst Music) that you choose and administer yourself. This Privacy Policy explains what data is processed when you use the app and how it is handled.

1. App Publisher

Amethyst Music is developed and maintained as an independent / open-source project. For any questions regarding this policy, you can contact us at the address listed in the "Contact" section below.

2. No Data Collection by the Publisher

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking tools (no Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, ad networks, etc.). The app's publisher does not collect, receive, or store any data about you on its own servers: Amethyst Music has no central server. All data described below stays solely between your device and the server you configure yourself.

3. Data Processed by the App

To function, the app processes locally (on your device) and exchanges with the server you configure the following data:

Data Purpose Storage location
Server address (URL) Connecting to your Purple Music / Amethyst Music instance Stored locally on the device (app preferences)
Server account username and password Authenticating with your server to access your music library Stored locally on the device; sent only to the server you configured
Music content (titles, metadata, cover art, lyrics) Displaying and playing your library Retrieved from your server; cached locally for offline playback if you enable downloads
Downloaded audio files Offline listening ("Downloads" mode) Local device storage only, until you delete them
Preferences (theme, equalizer, language, playlists) Personalizing your experience Stored locally on the device

None of this data is sent to the app's publisher or to any third party. It travels only between your device and the music server you have configured and are responsible for.

4. Permissions Used

  • Internet / Network state / Wi-Fi: needed to communicate with your server and stream or download music.
  • Foreground service (media playback): allows music to keep playing while the app is in the background.
  • Notifications: used to show playback controls and download progress.

The app does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, or microphone.

5. Connections to Self-Hosted Third-Party Servers

This app is designed to connect to the music server you choose (self-hosted by you or by a third party of your choice). The app's publisher has no control over, or visibility into, the data processed by that server. The privacy policy and security practices of that server are the responsibility of its administrator. We recommend checking with your server's administrator to understand how your data is handled there.

The app also offers an option to bypass HTTPS certificate errors in order to connect to servers using self-signed certificates. This option is provided for the convenience of technical users and should only be used with trusted servers, as it reduces protection against man-in-the-middle attacks.

6. Data Sharing

The app's publisher does not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties for advertising or commercial purposes. As described above, the only data transmitted is sent to the server you configure yourself.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Data stored locally (credentials, preferences, downloaded files) remains on your device until you manually delete it within the app (for example via "Log out" or by removing downloads) or until you uninstall the app, which automatically erases all associated local data.

8. Security

Login credentials are stored locally on your device and are only transmitted to the server you have configured. We recommend using a secure (HTTPS) connection to your server and only disabling certificate verification if you fully trust the server in question.

9. Children's Privacy

The app is not intended to knowingly collect data from children. As noted above, the app itself does not collect any data on third-party servers belonging to the publisher.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time, particularly as the app's features evolve. The date of the last update is shown at the top of this page.
